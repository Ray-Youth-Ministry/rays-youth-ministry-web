Sunday Youth Service

One service, every week — worship, the Word, and community.

☀️ Every Sunday — 4:00pm to 6:00pm · Jesus Power Centre, Mbale City

Sunday Evening Flow

What to expect

A guide to how the two hours generally run — timings may shift slightly week to week for special programs, guests, or altar moments.

4:00pm

Gathering & Praise

Doors open — youths gather, connect, and settle in with worship.

4:20pm

Worship

Extended time of praise and worship led by the youth worship team.

4:50pm

The Word

Teaching from scripture, addressing real questions youths are facing today.

5:30pm

Ministration & Altar Call

Prayer, response, and personal ministry time.

5:50pm

Fellowship & Announcements

Community time, upcoming events, and a send-off before 6:00pm.

Beyond Sunday

More ways to plug in

Ask any of our leaders on Sunday about small groups, outreach days, and upcoming special programs — new details are shared during announcements and on our social media pages.
